# Discover catalogue — rights evidence

Core 1.0 acceptance requires that every Discover sound has verified rights. This file is the
evidence register. Update it whenever a sound is added, replaced or removed.

## Official sounds (`sounds/*.mp3`, 26 items, `origin = official`, `license = snipring-standard`)

| Evidence | Where |
|---|---|
| Every sound is generated by code, by pure synthesis (oscillators, envelopes, filtered noise, generated reverb). No samples, loops, recordings or third-party audio are read. | `tools/make_sounds.py` (its only inputs are numpy/scipy math and a fixed random seed) |
| The audio files and the catalogue were produced by that generator and committed together | commit `3ad0a4a` (2026-10-07, "Phase 2: Discover sound library") |
| Catalogue entries carry `origin` and `license`; the database requires a copyright check before a sound can be published | `sounds/catalog.json`, `supabase/schema.sql` (`license`, `copyright_check`, publish check) |
| Licence text shown to users: free for personal use, as a ringtone or in the user's own videos | `discover.js` (`license`), generated sound pages (`tools/build_discover.py`) |

Check (re-run after any catalogue change):
```bash
python3 -c "import json,os;c=json.load(open('sounds/catalog.json'));print(len(c),{(x['origin'],x['license']) for x in c},all(os.path.exists(x['audio_path']) for x in c))"
```
Expected: `26 {('official', 'snipring-standard')} True`.

Limits of this evidence (stated honestly):
- The generator is not byte-reproducible (the MP3 encoder version changes the bytes), so the proof is
  the code plus the commit that added the files, not a hash match.
- Similarity to an existing melody was not checked by a musicologist. Most sounds are short tones,
  effects and generic patterns; a short manual listening review is still recommended before public launch.
- **Not legal advice.** The licence wording (`snipring-standard`) is part of the legal review of
  Terms/Privacy, which remains a launch blocker.

## Rule for any future sound
No sound enters Discover without a row here: source, author, licence, proof (file/commit/contract),
and who approved it. Community uploads stay off until moderation and copyright checks exist.
