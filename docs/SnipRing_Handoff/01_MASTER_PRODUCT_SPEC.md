# Master Product Specification
**Vision:** SnipRing is a global-first audio creation/discovery platform; SnipRing Studio is a separate professional AI-native music production experience at `studio.snipring.com`. Tagline: *Snip any sound. Create any sound. Make it yours.*
**Identity:** English default, Hebrew RTL supported; dark design, Electric Lime `#C7FF3D` for actions, Violet `#8B5CF6` for AI. Core mobile-first; Studio desktop-first with adaptive tablet/mobile UX.
**Principles:** value before signup; local-first guest audio; private by default; no automatic guest upload; shared identity with scoped authorization; controlled autonomy for AI with budgets and approval of sensitive actions; modular evolution without rebuilding current product.
**Approved long-term product areas:** Create, Discover, Sounds, Studio, My Library, AI Producer, Creator Network, Marketplace, rights/licensing, music distribution, marketing/growth, Academy, Live, Fan Economy, organizations/enterprise, developer APIs/SDK/plugins, workflow automation, audio intelligence, cross-platform apps, support, analytics, executive control center, media studio, creator services, global localization, enterprise security and quality platform.
**Studio vision:** professional multitrack DAW, Simple/Pro mode sharing one project, blank/template/import/recording/AI starts, Quick Generate plus editable Production Projects, MIDI/instruments, recording, mixing/mastering, permissioned AI singing, controlled AI agents, collaboration. Flattened generated audio must never be misrepresented as editable MIDI or original stems.
**Business vision:** Guest/Free/Pro/Studio, AI credits, marketplace commissions, creator monetization; prices, fees, quotas and provider costs remain undecided.
**Scope boundary:** Complete Core 1.0 FIRST. Future vision approval does not authorize coding future modules today.
